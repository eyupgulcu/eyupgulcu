### What I work on

Most of my projects are some mix of these:

- AI assistants on WhatsApp, email or the phone that answer from the business's own information and pass the conversation to a person when they aren't sure
- n8n and Make workflows, plus scheduled jobs, that connect inboxes, CRMs, calendars, payments and messaging
- Multi-tenant SaaS and CRMs on Next.js and Postgres, with roles, billing and reports

I keep a human in the loop for anything that sends money or makes a commitment, I log what the automation did, and I set things up in the client's own accounts.

### Products

**WhatsApp AI chatbot platform** (private). A business sets up its own WhatsApp bot in five steps: choose a sector template, upload a PDF to build the knowledge base, try the bot, connect a number and pick a plan. Staff can take over any conversation, send campaigns and see analytics. Next.js, Claude API, WhatsApp Cloud API and Evolution API, Clerk, Neon, iyzico.

**INSUREX, CRM for insurance agencies** (private). Tracks policies and sends WhatsApp renewal reminders 60, 30, 15 and 7 days before expiry. Also handles quotes, claims, commission checks, branches and an AI assistant for agents. Next.js, Postgres, Drizzle, background jobs, Gemini.

**AI review card** (private). NFC and QR cards that ask happy customers for a Google review and send complaints privately to the owner. New reviews get an AI-drafted reply, and owners get a weekly report on Telegram or WhatsApp. Next.js, Supabase, Gemini, Google Business Profile API, Lemon Squeezy.

**BeniYıka, mobile car-wash marketplace** (private). A customer posts a request, nearby approved washers send offers, the customer picks one and follows them on a map with an ETA. Runs on the web, as a PWA and as a native app, in Turkish and English. Next.js, Expo, Supabase with PostGIS and Realtime, MapLibre.

**Wherever Together**. Shows travel plans on a 3D globe so you can find people going to the same place in the same weeks and join their group chat. All access rules live in Postgres RLS. Next.js, Supabase, Three.js, Playwright, Vitest.

**[LeadHane](https://github.com/eyupgulcu/emlak-ofis-y-netim)**, a CRM for real estate offices. Listing site and lead pipeline. Claude matches buyers to listings, brokers get a WhatsApp summary every morning, and follow-ups go out on schedule. Next.js, Supabase, Claude API, WhatsApp.

**AI email assistant** (n8n). Sorts incoming Gmail, answers routine questions from an FAQ, leaves anything risky as a draft for a person, and logs each decision. n8n, OpenAI.
